# Acme Employee Handbook

## Welcome

Welcome to **Acme Corp**. This handbook covers working hours, leave policy, and security basics.

## Working hours

- Core hours: **10:00–16:00** (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh)
- Flexible start between 08:00 and 10:00
- Remote work is allowed up to **3 days/week** with manager approval

## Leave policy

| Type | Days / year | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Annual leave | 12 | Accrues monthly |
| Sick leave | 10 | Doctor note after 2 consecutive days |
| Unpaid leave | case-by-case | Requires HR approval |

Submit leave requests in **PeopleOS** at least 3 business days in advance.

## Security

1. Use company SSO for all internal tools.
2. Never share passwords or MFA codes.
3. Report phishing to `security@acme.example`.
4. Lock your laptop when away from desk.

## IT support

- Helpdesk: `#it-help` on Slack
- Email: `it@acme.example`
- Laptop issues: book a slot via Service Desk
