Acme Corp — New Hire Onboarding Checklist

Day 1
- Complete SSO account setup
- Join Slack workspaces: #general, #it-help, #people
- Read the Employee Handbook in the HR knowledge base
- Meet your buddy for a 30-minute intro call

Week 1
- Finish security awareness training
- Set up laptop disk encryption and MFA
- Review product overview docs for your team
- Schedule 1:1 with your manager

Week 2
- Shadow a support or customer call (if applicable)
- Create or join the relevant knowledge base
- Upload your first team note / runbook

Contacts
- People Ops: people@acme.example
- IT: it@acme.example
- Security: security@acme.example
